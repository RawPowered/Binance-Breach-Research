Third-party source screenshots are limited viewport excerpts retained with publisher, URL, capture time and context. They are not offered under a RawPowered license. Linking to or quoting the original source should retain its attribution and comply with the rights that apply to that material.

Binance and other referenced names and marks identify research subjects or source publishers. This independent project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Binance, the FBI, the named investigators or token issuers. The RawPowered stamp identifies the publisher of this research, not an official incident-response authority.

The editorial glass graphics used built-in AI image generation and were reviewed against the cited research. They are not screenshots of incident activity. Source screenshots and data charts are separately identified in the evidence archive.
