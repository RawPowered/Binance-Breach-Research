## How to read the evidence

Owner acknowledgment establishes what Binance says; an investigator's reconstruction is separately attributed. A government attribution is recorded as an official assessment. Directly inspected protocol documentation explains capabilities but does not prove who exploited them.

The financial rows are overlapping estimates, not amounts to sum. The timeline includes future commitments explicitly marked pending. Blank or day-only timestamps are intentional.

The source register provides links to owner notices, the FBI alert, original tracing work, protocol documentation and independent reporting. It also identifies incomplete page access, mutable reports and unresolved source timestamps.

## Scope

This is an extensive targeted compilation of publicly available material, not a final postmortem or independently audited loss/recovery ledger. Limited source-page viewport excerpts are archived with provenance; full articles, leaked records and attacker tools are not reproduced. No independent blockchain replay or reserve audit was performed.
